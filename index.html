FAQ

Can I apply if I don't live in a major city?

Yes. You can apply for a Cambodia visa remotely. Document submission and consultations take place online, so your location does not matter.

What documents do I need to get started?

To get started, you only need your passport and personal details. Your manager will then clarify your situation and prepare a tailored document checklist.

How long does the process take?

Processing times depend on the service and your circumstances. We’ll provide an estimated timeline after reviewing your case during the initial consultation.

Can everything be done remotely, without being there in person?

Yes, in most cases the process is fully remote. If your case requires extra steps or an in‑person visit, we’ll let you know in advance and advise you on the best option.

What happens if my visa or registration is denied?

We’ll review the reason for the refusal, help correct your documents and guide you through the next steps. Your manager will explain the process for your case.

Do you work with citizens of all countries?

We work with clients from most countries. Restrictions may apply to certain jurisdictions. Your manager can clarify whether they affect your case.

What payment methods do you accept?

Several payment methods are available, including bank transfers and cryptocurrency. Your manager will provide the current options and payment details when you apply.

What exactly is covered in the package price?

What’s included depends on your chosen package. Before we begin, your manager will explain the cost and answer your questions, so you know exactly what you’re paying for.
